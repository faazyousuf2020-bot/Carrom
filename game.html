How to play
  • Slide the striker along your baseline with the slider, or drag the striker itself.
  • Touch anywhere on the board and pull back like a catapult. The arrow shows direction and power. Let go to shoot.
  • You play white from the bottom. Pocket your coin and you shoot again.
  • Queen: pocket it only after one of your coins, then cover it by pocketing your coin in the next shot.
  • Shots must go forward. To reach coins behind your baseline, bounce off a cushion.
  • Striker in a pocket is a foul. You lose the turn and one of your coins goes back to the centre.
