You play from the bottom against the computer at the top.

How to play
  • Slide the striker along your baseline with the slider, or drag the striker itself.
  • Touch anywhere on the board and pull back like a catapult. Small pulls give delicate shots; long pulls give full power. Let go to shoot.
  • Turn on Precise to lock your aim, nudge it a fraction of a degree, then tap Shoot.
  • Pocket your own coin and you shoot again. Shots must go forward; use a cushion for coins behind your baseline.
  • Queen: take it after one of your coins, then cover it with your next shot.
  • The break must touch a coin (3 tries). The breaker plays white; the break alternates each board.
  • Coins on or behind your baseline can't be hit directly; reach them off a cushion.
  • Fouls (striker in a pocket, potting the opponent's last coin, or your last coin before the queen is covered) cost you a coin. If the striker goes in together with your own coin, the coins come back but you keep the turn (ICF law 73).
  • A board scores the opponent's coins left plus 3 for the queen (only up to 21 points). Games go to 25 or 29 points, or 8 boards.
